Master Download Manager is installed.

Getting started
  * Click "+ Add URL" (or press Ctrl+V in the window) and paste a video or
    playlist link, a direct file link, a magnet link, or open a .torrent file.
  * Browser extension: open Settings -> Browser Integration in the app for the
    extension folders and your security token.

Everything the app needs (media engine, FFmpeg, JavaScript runtime, torrent
engine) is included. It only uses the Internet for the downloads you start.

Your settings, history and logs are stored in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MasterDownloadManager
and are kept if you uninstall.

Third-party components and their licenses are listed in the "licenses"
folder and in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md inside the installation folder.
Only download content you have the right to download.
